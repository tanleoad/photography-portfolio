The Photographer

About

Abu Dhabi, UAE

Tanleo_

Tania Khan is a photographer, visual storyteller, and SonyMEA Speaker, born & raised in Abu Dhabi, specializing in street and architectural imagery with a strong focus on cinematic color grading. Her work explores the relationship between light, mood, and color, transforming everyday scenes into intentional visual narratives.
