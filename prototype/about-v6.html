Tania Khan is a photographer, visual storyteller, and SonyMEA Speaker, born & raised in Abu Dhabi, specializing in street and architectural imagery with a strong focus on cinematic color grading.

Her work explores the relationship between light, mood, and colour, transforming everyday scenes into intentional visual narratives.

Exhibited and featured — Fulle Galleria, International Exhibition, Argentina; Spaces of Light, Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Competition; Qasr Al Hosn, Official Instagram Feature; Cultural Foundation, Official Instagram Feature; Film Abu Dhabi Commission, Official Instagram Feature; Ethara / Abu Dhabi Grand Prix, Corporate Suite Photography; What’s On Abu Dhabi, Published Feature.
